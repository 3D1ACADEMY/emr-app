## Full Description (max 4,000 characters)

```
CEMS (Clinical Emergency Management System) is an offline-first emergency reference app built for aesthetic medicine professionals, including dermatologists, plastic surgeons, nurses, and injector clinicians.

Developed by 3D Rejuvenation Academy and Dr. Amr Ismail, MD, CEMS puts critical complication protocols, dosing calculators, and anatomical references on your device — no clinic Wi-Fi or cellular coverage required.

FEATURES

• Vascular Occlusion Protocol — step-by-step reversal with hyaluronidase dosing calculator
• Danger Zone Atlas — interactive facial anatomy map with tappable high-risk injection zones
• Anaphylaxis & Local Anesthetic Toxicity protocols with weight-based calculators
• Acute Hematoma, Vasovagal Syncope, Herpes Simplex Outbreak, and Infection guidance
• Incident Log — document complications with audio notes, photos, and PDF export
• Emergency Facility Locator — find nearby hospitals and HBOT centers, online or offline
• Patient Context — quick pre-procedure patient data for safer dosing decisions
• Secure, encrypted on-device storage with biometric/PIN access

OFFLINE-FIRST & PRIVATE

All clinical content is bundled in the app. Incident and patient data stays encrypted on your device using expo-secure-store. No patient identifiers or PHI are transmitted to external servers.

EDUCATIONAL REFERENCE ONLY

CEMS is a clinical decision-support and educational tool. It does not replace physician judgment, specialist consultation, or institutional protocols. Always follow your local medico-legal and regulatory requirements.

Built for 3D Rejuvenation Academy | Dr. Amr Ismail, MD
https://www.3drejuvenationcode.com
```
